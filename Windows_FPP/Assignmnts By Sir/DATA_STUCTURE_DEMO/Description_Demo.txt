Description :
‘A Birthday Gift’, is an animation sequence of a very common birthday gift, a ‘Greeting Card’. Although simple, it brings a story of its own, short but sweet.
A story of a loving Father nurturing its Creation. Never stopping however bad the circumstances get. A story of a true gentleman and a living LEGEND.

Technology Used :
OpenGL Programmable Pipeline
OpenAL
macOS Catalina 10.15.7
